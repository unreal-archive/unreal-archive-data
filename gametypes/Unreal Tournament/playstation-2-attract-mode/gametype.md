This gametype adapts the attract mode/demo Deathmatch mode from the PlayStation 2 version
of Unreal Tournament that offers a unique perspective on spectating bots.

Original code/assets by Epic Games/Infogrames and provided by slade, adapted by
AlCapowned.

## Installation

To install, put `UTPS2AttractMode.u` and `UTPS2AttractMode.int` in your Unreal
Tournament's System folder. Pick PlayStation 2 Attract Mode from the list of gametypes.
