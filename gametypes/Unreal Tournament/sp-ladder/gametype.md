Here is the new single player ladder for Unreal Tournament.

Its main features are

- Optional use of mutators (you can turn thhem off)
- Last Man Standing Ladder
- Three & Four teams in Domination laddder
- Challenge ladder has multiple game types like assault, ctf, team game, deathmatch etc.
- There is a match between Loque & you in Morbias][ in instaGib mod
- There is a match between Loque & Xan in Hyperblast in which u can spectate
- A new team lead by Xan

## Installation

- Extract the two files `UCMenu.u` & `UCMenu.int` into the `UnralTournament/System` folder.
- Start the game and from Mod Menu choose Unreal Championship.

The mod menu offers Start Unreal Championship, Resume Saved Championship and Reset
Unreal Tournament, along with a per-ladder switch for mutators covering the DM, CTF,
LMS, DOM and Challenge ladders.
