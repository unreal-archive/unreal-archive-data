Salvage Scoop will be a free a total conversion for Unreal Gold. Set in a completely alternative
universe as the original character in Unreal escapes, only to be led to a portal to be teleported
elsewhere.

The journey wouldn't be so easy as you'll take control of the original characters from Unreal but
welding different weapons which are similar to the original weapons from Unreal Gold. The player
would also come across artefacts which will help them during their journey.

- All Weapons transformed
- Different Single Player Story
- Different Enemies

## Salvage Storm Demo for Unreal Gold

Salvage Storm Demo for Unreal Gold, play with 4 weapons, 4 maps, different enemies and other cool
features. See a total conversion for Unreal Gold at the fullest.

## Installation

Extract files into the following folders:

- .utx to textures
- all system files to system folder
- .umx to music
- .unr to maps

Run SalvageDemo.lnk to run the mod and enjoy.
