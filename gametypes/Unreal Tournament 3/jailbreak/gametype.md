Two teams face off to sentence the other to death!

Frag an enemy player, and you send them to jail. Get them all into jail and they will be
executed in all kinds of crazy ways and your team scores! To avoid being captured, find the
release switch in the enemy base to free your team!

## Installation

Extract all files to your `My Games\Unreal Tournament 3\UTGame\Published\CookedPC\` folder.
Requires UT3 patch 2.1 and the Titan Pack.

## Release history

Jailbreak for UT3 was shown publicly as a Beta Demo on 31 August 2009, entered in round 4 of
the Make Something Unreal Contest where it took 5th place for best FPS mod. Beta 2 followed on
17 January 2010, dropping JB-Jaques_Birthday and adding JB-Yaijm. It is the last UT3 release -
the team returned to finishing Jailbreak 2004, and the further UT3 version they spoke about in
2012 never appeared.
