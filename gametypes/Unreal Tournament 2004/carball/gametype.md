Carball is a user modification (or mod, for short) for Unreal Tournament 2004 (UT2004)
that introduces a new gametype involving vehicles and a giant ball in a soccer/football
like competition. Players drive dune buggy like vehicles with the goal of knocking a
large, indestructible, bouncing ball into a goal. These vehicles are capable of not only
flipping, jumping, and other acrobatics, but also of using various power-ups to change
their speed, visibility, and weaponry.

The Carball mod is unique in that it changes the game's focus from killing other players
to driving, teamwork and having fun with physics. Weapons can affect other players, but
are more often used on the ball. Chaos and unpredictability are common in Carball games,
even ones with great team work and communication from both sides. Far more so than most
mods, Carball will often make you laugh as much as it will make you jeer and cheer.

The most important thing to think of when playing is teamwork. You must work with your
other teammates to set up the score just like you would in real soccer.

## Game play

Gameplay is simple in theory: players use vehicles to attempt to place a ball into the
goal area of an opposing team. The team that scores the most number of goals at the end
of a match is the winning team. In practise, that is easier said then done and that is
where the fun comes from.

Power-ups are available to enhance a vehicle's abilities. Drive over a power-up and it is
added to your inventory (or in the case of the repair power-up it takes effect
immediately). You may hold up to three power-ups at any time and activate them when you
wish. Each power-up has a primary and an alt-fire mode.

All Carball vehicles are capable of acrobatics - they can do wheelies, jump, flip into
the air, and point their nose up or down.

## Power-ups

**Repair Kit** - This Power-Up will repair 50 points worth of damage to your vehicle.

**Nitro Boost** - Provides a speed boost, but reduces control. Alt-Fire causes the car to
jump straight up.

**Goo Pile** - Drops a bunch of slime globs that causes damage. Alt-Fire lobs the slime
forward. The longer you hold down the Alt-Fire button, the further the slime is lobbed.

**Pocket Rocket** - Fires a powerful rocket. It no longer does damage; it only knocks
people around.

**Invisibility** - Makes your vehicle invisible.

**Flip-Mine** - Drops a mine which flips vehicles that drive over it.

**Nuke** - A large area explosion.

**Portable Repulse Field Generator** - Primary fire does a repulse effect only; alt-fire
functions as a damage shield.

**Electro-Pulse Generator** - Primary fire shoots a beam of energy that can knock other
vehicles around, and to a lesser extent, the ball. Alt-fire fires an Electromagnetic
Pulse (EMP) that will shoot out from the front of your car. If a car is hit by this
pulse, it will be unable to drive for several seconds.

## Customization

You can customize your car with custom license plates, skins, and wheels.

## Installation

Once you have installed a full retail version of UT2004 with the latest patches and other
updates on your computer system, you can install Carball. Activate the UMOD or expand the
ZIP archive to the root directory of your UT2004 install.

After a successful installation, Carball should appear as an entry in your "Game Type"
drop down menu and can be selected just like you would select an Onslaught, Capture the
Flag or an Assault game.

## The community

Unreal Tournament 2004 (UT2004) has produced many user modifications (mods) that are more
high profile than Carball, but very few with players as dedicated as the crowd of Carball
regulars ("The Carball Community"). You will be hard pressed to find a mod for any game
that inspires the use of the word "addicted" or outbursts of laughter from its players as
much as Carball does.

The most important tip of all comes from a quote made by the player who goes by the handle
Gruelsicle: "Don't take five minutes off of your life over Carball."
