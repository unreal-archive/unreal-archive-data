Dodge Professional Modification (DPM) is currently a single player trick jumping mod for UT2004. DPM does not alter the physics, weapons, or jump capabilities of UT2004. This allows players to apply the skills learned while playing DPM to other UT2004 game types.

The goal of DPM is to finish specially designed trick jumping maps as quickly as possible. A timer tracks exactly how long it takes for a player to complete one of the DPM maps. Demos are automatically recorded when the player is ready and these demo can be uploaded to our site. These demos will then be sorted and ranked by completion time to form a world-wide player ranking.
