## Gateways

Just in case you aren't familiar with our masterpiece that's spawned so many imitators, this carnage fest pits you against a handful of very tough Interdimentional Gateways that produce an unlimited stream of monsters for as long as they are active. Destroy the Gateways and you stop the monsters. And our Monsters can't see through walls, unlike Epic's clone, "Invasion". You can use these lulls in combat to load up and plan your attack. It takes more then a quick Trigger finger to survive this lot. But it can be done, and more importantly, you CAN actually WIN this game!

U4E Gateways is available in two flavors, normal and Extra-Nutty, "Vehicle Gateways"! Vehicle Gateways allows you to use Onslaught maps and vehicles to aid you in your assault on the endless monsters. Do you think a Tank can easily stop a rampaging horde of ticked off monsters? You'd be surprised how fast a swarm of Flies can cut through armor!

## Monster Master

Capture Console: This weapon has a lot of features, which cause an advance interface.

The 1st capture round is indicated with the Black star in the top-middle. In this round there will be enough monsters for everybody to capture. Also you can not lose lives in this round. Nor can you attack an enemy. After 3 or 4 minutes you switch to round 2.

The 2nd capture round is indicated with the Red star in the top-middle. In this round you got 1 life, if you die your out. There are no longer random monsters, only other players can launch there monsters to come and kill you. Use the monsters you captured in round 1 to defend yourselves and kill your enemies. Last man standing wins.

## Build CTF

The meaning of build CTF is that you and your team can defend your CTF-base by making constructions to make it harder for the opposing team to capture your flag.

There are 2 rounds in bCTF each game, the first round is called "Build-round" the second "CTF-round".

In this round you must build your base to defend it. Maybe your thinking right now: I'll just build around my flag so that no-one can touch it! But you must be aware while building that you must also return the opposing-team's flag to your own flag without using the translocator. Make it as easy as possible for your own team to return the flag and as hard for the opposing team. So don't seal off all entrances.

## U4E2D

This gametype was first made for UT2003 known as 2D Tournament by k0lpA. It is a gametype where you can play in a 2D sidescrolling evironment which adds a whole different dimention to playing. Now U4E has took over this great mod and you can now play it in UT2004.

In U4E2D you can do anything that you could in 2D Tournament: playing in a 2D sidescrolling environment, but it can do more. In U4E2D you can not only play in a sidescrolling environment, but also in a topdown environment. It also got more customizing options for players to customize their way of playing.
